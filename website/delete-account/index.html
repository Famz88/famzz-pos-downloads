Account & Data Deletion

Use this page to request deletion of your FamZz POS account and associated personal data.

Submit a deletion request

Email us from the email address registered to your FamZz POS account. Include your registered email address and, if applicable, company name. Do not send your password, session token, payment-card details or offline passphrase.

Request account deletion

What happens next

We may need to verify that the request comes from the account holder before completing deletion. Once verified, we will process the request within a reasonable period and confirm when it is completed.

What is deleted

For an account-deletion request, we delete or anonymize the account's associated personal data and authentication information where applicable. Active sessions are invalidated as part of account removal.

Company and transaction records

FamZz POS is a business system. Orders, financial records, audit records and other company workspace data may belong to the company operating the POS rather than to an individual staff member. Some records may need to be retained for legitimate legal, accounting, security, fraud-prevention or regulatory purposes. Where retention is required, we limit retained information to the applicable purpose.

Offline copies

After your request is completed, remove FamZz POS offline data from devices you control by signing out, clearing offline access or uninstalling the Android application. Unsynchronized business transactions should be synchronized with the company before removing local data.
