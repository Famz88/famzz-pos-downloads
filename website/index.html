KNOW BEFORE YOU START

Your questions, answered.

Does FamZz POS need an internet connection?

Yes. The current online POS needs an active internet connection to sign in, load company data and save transactions. Plan reliable connectivity for your service.

How do owners and staff sign in?

Owners and administrators use their FamZz account through Company Owner / Admin. Staff use the Company ID, username and password supplied by their company administrator.

Can I manage more than one company or outlet?

The company portal supports multiple companies, with separate company workspaces. Outlet access is assigned to staff, and the number of outlets and active users is governed by each company license.

Can a cashier close the day?

Yes, when an administrator enables the dedicated Day Close permission on their role. Running and held orders must be resolved, and any open cash shift must be closed and reconciled first.

What happens if a working date is behind?

When an authorized user opens the app without an active draft, past working dates can catch up to the current local date. Open orders and cash shifts block catch-up until reconciled. This is session-triggered, not an unattended midnight service.

Does it charge cards or connect to every printer?

The POS records the selected payment method; it does not automatically charge a card terminal. Kitchen tickets and receipts use browser printing. Confirm printer compatibility and any payment or integration requirements with us before purchase.

How do I activate or renew my license?

Contact us with your company details and required user and outlet allowances. FamZz confirms the terms and manages license activation or renewal centrally. Staff cannot issue or change company licenses.

How do I request support or discuss integrations?

Email your company name, a description of the issue, and relevant hardware or integration requirements. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or session tokens. Integration availability and setup are confirmed individually.
